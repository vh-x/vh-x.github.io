About me Link to heading

Hi, I’m a self-taught web developer from Taiwan who’s been into tech and hacking around with code long before I even knew what a “developer” meant. I love figuring out how things work and making them better.

I’m all about learning beyond the fundamentals, exploring some low-level stuff, and diving into computer science concepts—not just sticking to the latest trends in tech.

I spent several years in general tech support before transitioning to web development.

This blog is where I share what I’m building, learning, and thinking about in the tech world. Stick around—there’s always something new on the way.
